01 · Enabling Research Without Borders

Global Academic Exposure

Students’ International Relations Cell of IIT Kharagpur empowers students to pursue meaningful research internships across the globe. These opportunities provide an immersive experience in diverse academic cultures, promoting collaboration with renowned faculty and researchers. Internships abroad help students cultivate critical thinking, cross-cultural fluency, and leadership abilities. With each experience, students return not only with technical expertise but also with the confidence and insight to contribute to global academic and industrial landscapes. Our aim is to build globally-aware innovators who are prepared to lead in an interconnected world.

02 · Streamlined Applications for International Training

Foreign Internship Facilitation

The Foreign Training Program at IIT Kharagpur has been introduced to provide students with easier access to prestigious international internship opportunities. Securing overseas internships often involves a lengthy application process, numerous formalities, and uncertainty regarding reliable guidance. To address these challenges, the program simplifies procedures by offering centralized support, structured mentorship, and access to curated resources that reduce administrative hurdles. This allows students to focus their energy on academic excellence, innovative research, and meaningful professional growth. Beyond academic exposure, the program ensures that students experience diverse cultural and professional environments by engaging with reputed global institutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is it different applying through a portal as compared to applying through personal mailing?
The FTP portal streamlines the process by connecting you directly with vetted professors and institutions actively looking for interns. It saves you from sending hundreds of cold emails and offers structured guidance.
Does CG matter while applying in these projects?
While CGPA is an important metric for academic evaluation, professors often look at your overall profile, research experience, and skill set relevant to the project.
If I have been selected for a project through FTP, am I eligible to apply for the upcoming projects in the portal?
Generally, to ensure fair opportunities for all students, once you accept an offer through the FTP, you might be restricted from applying to further projects in the same cycle.
After getting finalized for a project, can I back out from the same?
Please contact the Students' International Relations Cell at irc@iitkgp.ac.in for guidance on this.
Where can I find the ERP generated transcript?
You can generate and download your official transcript from your ERP dashboard under the academic section.
Does Students’ International Relations Cell provide any help while applying for a visa?
Please contact the Students' International Relations Cell at irc@iitkgp.ac.in for guidance on this.

Students' International Relations Cell

Students' International Relations Cell (SIRC) is a student body working under the aegis of the Office of International Relations (OIR). It works to promote the exchange of students, faculties, and ideas between IIT Kharagpur and the international partners and establishing the globalization of IIT Kharagpur.
